## VNM Direct Drive Premier — Review Notes

### Quick Reference
- **Review Status**: Not started
- **Last Updated**: 2026-03-07

### Core Takeaways
- 13 Nm direct drive wheelbase positioned as entry VNM model; €699 incl. taxes (compact 130×130×228 mm, 11.6 kg).
- Strong value: small form factor, multiple mounting options (front/side/bottom), VNM QR base side included; slip ring + wheel-side QR available separately (€35 for wheel-side QR).
- High-quality FFB with detailed feel; experimental Telemetry Force Feedback for iRacing, AC/ACC, rF2, Le Mans Ultimate.
- Slip ring enables USB passthrough/clean cable management; best with non-high-speed USB wheels (may disconnect on display wheels like VoCore).

### Specs (Wheelbase)
- **Product**: VNM Direct Drive Premier (SKU: VNM-DD01PRE)
- **Motor**: 5 pole-pairs smooth motor; direct drive
- **Encoder**: 23-bit
- **Max Torque**: 13 Nm
- **Power Supply**: 300 W (appearance may vary by batch)
- **Dimensions**: 130×130×228 mm (without QR base side)
- **Weight**: 11.6 kg
- **Packaging**: 47×35×24 cm
- **QR**: VNM QR base side included; wheel-side QR €35
- **Emergency Stop**: EMC stop button regular (Superior optional)
- **Slip Ring**: Included for USB passthrough; uses front USB-C routed to rear

### Compatibility & Features
- **Platform**: PC; broad game compatibility
- **Telemetry Force Feedback (experimental)**: iRacing, Assetto Corsa, Assetto Corsa Competizione, rFactor 2, Le Mans Ultimate
- **Mounting**: Front, side, bottom mounting options
  - Front mount thickness must be ≤10 mm; thicker requires spacer or thin USB-C connector
- **USB passthrough**: Slip ring replaces fixed wiring for full rotation without cable wrap

### Slip Ring Notes
- **Purpose**: Rotary coupling for power/signal/data between stationary/rotating parts; integrated with VNM QR
- **Signal path**: Wheel → VNM QR → VNM Slipring → PC (via front USB-C to rear)
- **Best use**: Non-USB high-speed (480 MHz) wheels; stable for non-screen wheels
- **Limitation**: Wheels with displays may see occasional disconnections; VNM improving
- **User guidance**: Consider USB speed needs before purchase; mind front-mount thickness limit

### Value Positioning
- Smallest VNM DD; targeted at high performance on lower budget while retaining build quality

### Pricing
- **Wheelbase**: €699 incl. taxes
- **Slip Ring**: €80
- **Wheel-side QR**: €35

### First Impressions (Lawrence)
- **Unboxing/Packaging**: Minimalist, plain printing; early packaging rough (bubble envelopes, generic cables). QR wheel-side included but presentation unpolished.
- **Hardware/Build**: Base feels heavy/compact; huge QR shaft with USB-C at front for passthrough; front/side/bottom mount options; emergency stop included (power cutoff style). Quick release functional but industrial; large rotational mass but free-spinning.
- **Setup friction**: Required two USB connections (base + wheel). Firmware update/flash via VNM Flash (needed to click cart icon to fetch firmware); initial lack of FFB until max torque mode enabled and firmware flashed—took >1 hour with Discord help.
- **Software**: VNM software shows temps; default smoothness filter high; telemetry FF plugins available; LED config possible (uses SimHub). Needed to disable filters/dampers for feel; crash protection aggressive.
- **FFB first drive (AC benchmark)**: Once updated + max torque, strong detail and intuitive self-aligning behavior; aggressive under/oversteer cues, good curb detail after reducing smoothing. Felt less than expected 13 Nm initially; improvement after filter tweaks. Grain/noise reduced with slight damper. Good fidelity; torque feels like toned-down higher-Nm base; large 330 mm rim reduces felt torque.
- **Comparisons**: Feels better than Moza R12; Logitech Pro felt more powerful; Asetek La Prima cited as best-in-range reference; Simucube SC3 Pro still stronger (twice power). Wants smaller/formula rim to assess detail.
- **Apex Wheel plate**: Round rim; bright LEDs (rainbow by default). Nice detented rotaries/thumbs; 7-way hats slightly loose. Clutch paddles short throw, adjustable; shifters damped/quiet; lighting configurable in SimHub. DIY feel with extension cables/pin options but flexible; can run as USB on other bases.
- **Misc**: Telemetry FF in AC unclear/not working; will try iRacing. Will use for Formula V scholarship. Recommends buying via resellers (e.g., Trak Racer) to avoid shipping/customs from Vietnam.

# HandsOnPerformance Review Notes

## EMC e-Master Cylinder Handbrake

### Product Overview
- **Full Name**: HandsOnPerformance EMC (e-Master Cylinder)
- **Type**: Electronic master cylinder for hydraulic handbrakes
- **Price**: €250 (EMC unit only, handle not included)
- **Purpose**: Emulates real driving experience by replacing hydraulic master cylinder with load cell system
- **Compatibility**: Works with any race, rally, or drift hydraulic handbrake designed for Willwood-type master cylinder

### What's Included
- EMC load cell assembly (Willwood cylinder shape)
- USB cable
- Control unit (hardwired into side, nice casing - not 3D printed)
- Elastomer cups
- Additional elastomers
- Mounting bolts (for attaching handle)
- **Note**: Handle NOT included

### Build & Design
- **Load Cell**: 100kg load cell (can handle 200kg, reaches 100% at 100kg)
- **Cup System**: Elastomer cups (similar to Mecca Cup, Mecca Evo, Fanatec latest pedals)
- **Cup Benefit**: Prevents elastomers from deforming during travel
- **Construction**: High-grade components
- **Control Unit**: Hardwired, branded HOP casing (not 3D printed)
- **Shape**: Exact shape of Willwood cylinder for direct replacement
- **USB Connection**: Load cell deflector with USB output to PC

### Handle Options Tested
#### Budget Option - Universal Cage Handbrake
- **Price**: ~€60-70 (available on eBay)
- **Total Cost**: ~€320 with EMC unit (plus shipping)
- **Type**: Very universal cage handbrake handle
- **Quality**: Functional but basic

#### Premium Option - Wise Fab Handbrake
- **Price**: €270
- **Total Cost**: €520+ with EMC unit
- **Features**: Cage to stop lockout at end, prevents hard pull on cylinder
- **Quality**: "Absolutely stunning", "gorgeous", "absolutely beautiful"
- **Appearance**: Another level above universal cage handles
- **Note**: Loaned for review, had to be returned

### Installation & Mounting
- Represents only the cylinder part of handbrake setup
- Mounts exactly like hydraulic master cylinder would in real car
- Use same handbrake in rig as in real car (important for some users)
- Bracket is exactly what would be used in real race car
- USB cable comes out of back of load cell unit

### Feel & Performance
#### Positives
- **Best Handbrake Tested**: "It is the best feeling handbrake, to be honest"
- **Progressive Feel**: Very progressive feeling
- **Initial Pull**: Quite light at start (~0-15%)
- **Mid-Range**: Very steady from 15% onwards
- **End Range**: Little bit heavier towards the end
- **Travel**: Lots of travel without elastomer deformation (thanks to cup system)
- **Natural Feel**: Feels as good as any hydraulic handbrake used in real drift cars
- **Intuitive**: "Feels so intuitive with this handbrake"
- **Real-World Comparison**: At least as good as real hydraulic handbrakes
- **Cup System Success**: Prevents elastomer deformation even with extensive travel
- **Long Handle Benefit**: Beautiful long handle helps with travel

#### Characteristics
- **Not Light**: "There's a big pull on that, guys. It feels very beefy"
- **Not Casual**: "If you want a light, casual handbrake, this is not it"
- **Leverage**: Quite a bit of leverage on the Wise Fab setup
- **Force Required**: Would need to be "an absolute beast" to hit 100kg
- **Ideal Weight**: Handbrake should be quick and snappy, lighter usually better
